Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2, 2026

The TenJ Lab website (tenjlab.org) is an informational site about the TenJ Lab at the UCLA Joe C. Wen School of Nursing. This policy explains what information the site and our social media tools handle.

Information we collect

The website is a static site. You do not need to create an account or provide personal information to browse it, and we do not use advertising or third-party tracking cookies. The on-site search runs entirely in your browser. Our web host (GitHub Pages) may record standard technical logs, such as IP addresses, as part of serving the site.

Instagram and Meta

The TenJ Lab operates its own Instagram account (@tenjlab). We use Meta’s Instagram Graph API only to publish our own lab content, such as posts and stories, to that account. Our application does not collect, store, or process information from other Instagram users or from visitors to this website.

Links to other sites

Our site and posts may link to external websites, for example journal articles, university pages, or partner studies. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to Dr. Dante Anthony Tolentino at tenjlab@sonnet.ucla.edu.
